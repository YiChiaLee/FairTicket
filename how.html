EDUCATION

How It Works

A transparent, fair system designed so everyone has an equal shot at tickets.

Real-Name System × Lottery × Phased Release

Each participant must complete identity verification using a government ID or passport. Each individual is allowed to register only once. Through a multi-stage lottery system, tickets are distributed fairly to those who genuinely want to attend.

▣

Real-Name System

Identity verified via gov ID or passport. One person, one entry.

⌘

Lottery Draw

Winners selected randomly — no speed advantage, no bots.

▤

Phased Release

Forfeited tickets re-enter rolling rounds so nothing goes to waste.

PROCESS

Full Ticketing Timeline

▣
Stage 1

Registration Period

Register after completing identity verification. One entry per person.

▭
Stage 2

First Lottery Period

A five-day period for customers to register.

↻
Stage 3

First Lottery Result & Payment

Winners are notified and must complete payment within the deadline.

◎
Stage 4

Rolling Lottery Period

Unclaimed tickets enter a 12-hour rolling lottery cycle.

▣
Stage 5

Final First-Come, First-Served Sale

Remaining tickets are released in limited quantities under real-name verification.

☆
Stage 6

Event Day

ID checked at entry. Enjoy the event!

IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Why Is Identity Verification Required?

◉

Each ID/passport can only be used once, preventing bulk ticket purchases.

⌂

IDs are checked at entry; tickets are non-transferable.

▤

Personal data is stored in compliance with data protection regulations and deleted after the event.
